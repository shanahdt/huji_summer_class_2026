Discussion Question 1: Return to Day 1#

Discussion Question 2: The Encoding Chain#

Beregovski heard musicians → transcribed → typeset → Malin/Shanahan encoded kern → you loaded Python.

At which step was the most significant information lost? Does it matter which step, or does each loss compound the others?

Discussion Question 4: The Missing Corpus#

Why does a large, computationally ready corpus of Israeli folk music not exist?

  • What collection decisions would have to be made?

  • Who would be qualified to make them?

  • Is the absence a problem to be solved, or a condition to be understood?

Discussion Question 5: Spotify Was librosa at Scale#
